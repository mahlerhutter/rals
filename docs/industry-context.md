# Industry Context: Renewable Energy M&A

Renewable asset M&A (Mergers and Acquisitions) is plagued by unstructured data. A 100 MW solar plant is a complex financial and technical object, yet it is traditionally traded using PDFs, bespoke Excel models, and unstructured data rooms.

## The Pain Point
Every developer and advisor uses a different format for their "Teaser" and "Information Memorandum". When an institutional buyer (like an infrastructure fund or IPP) receives dealflow, they spend weeks manually extracting data (MW capacity, PPA strike price, land lease terms) just to decide if they want to bid.

## The AI Opportunity
Large Language Models (LLMs) are perfect for reading dealflow, but they hallucinate on unstructured financial PDFs. If the industry adopts a machine-readable standard (RALS), an LLM acting as a buyer's agent can instantly parse 100 assets, run them against a strict investment mandate, and output a prioritized shortlist.

## Why YAML?
RALS uses YAML 1.2 instead of JSON-LD or XML because renewable deal teams consist of finance professionals, not just engineers. YAML is easily readable by a human M&A associate, easily generated by an LLM, and strictly parseable by an automated system.

## Confidentiality is Paramount
Unlike real estate, renewable energy transactions are highly confidential. Revealing the specific coordinates of an asset or the name of the SPV before an NDA is signed can derail a transaction. This is why RALS implements a strict 3-tier confidentiality model (`public`, `nda_required`, `final_shortlist`), ensuring the standard respects the reality of the M&A process.
