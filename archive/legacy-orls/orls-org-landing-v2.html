An open standard for renewable energy asset listings.

ORLS specifies a machine-readable YAML format for describing renewable energy assets offered for sale, refinancing, or partnership. It enables sellers, advisors, and prospective buyers — including AI agents — to discover, parse, and pre-qualify utility-scale assets without proprietary data rooms or bespoke spreadsheet templates.

What an ORLS document looks like

Below is a fragment of a reference document — an operating solar PV asset in Spain. Tier markers in comments indicate which fields appear in the public teaser and which require NDA-gated access.

# Project Helios — fragment orls_version: "0.1" document_id: "orls:01H8XY7K9M2N3P4Q5R6S7T8U9V" identity: asset_name: "Project Helios" # [P] asset_type: solar_pv # [P] lifecycle_stage: operating # [P] country: ES # [P] total_capacity_mw: 87.5 # [P] seller_legal_entity: "Helios Iberica..." # [N] financials: asking_price: # [F] indicated_range_eur_low: 78000000 indicated_range_eur_high: 92000000

[P] public · [N] nda_required · [F] final_shortlist

Specification structure

An ORLS document consists of seven top-level sections plus a metadata header. All seven are required for a complete profile; the public teaser subset is derived by tier-filtering.

§ 1
identity
Asset name, type, lifecycle stage, jurisdiction, capacity, transaction type, seller entity.
v0.1 stable
§ 2
technical
Equipment configuration, production estimates, resource assessment, performance metrics.
v0.1 stable
§ 3
grid_offtake
Grid connection, offtake contracts, capacity programs, merchant exposure, certificates.
v0.1 stable
§ 4
financials
Capex, opex, revenue history, debt structure, tax position, valuation reference.
v0.1 stable
§ 5
compliance
Permits, land/water rights, environmental assessments, EU Taxonomy, jurisdiction-specific.
v0.1 stable
§ 6
operating_history
Production history, availability, major events, service arrangements, SCADA access.
→ polymorphic in v0.2
§ 7
process
Process type, timeline, closing conditions, data room reference, access endpoint.
v0.1 stable

Governance

    

      ORLS is currently maintained under a Benevolent Dictator For Life model with explicit transition to a Steering Committee planned upon five independent implementations. The RFC process is open to anyone; ratification authority sits with the editor until the Steering Committee transition.
    

    

      Major version updates are capped at one per 18 months. Minor updates may occur as needed. Patches are issued for clarifications and bug fixes only.
